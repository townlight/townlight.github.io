Inside the repository

What the Legal foundation contains

Cited record search

Search city records with citations and look up prior actions, with privilege-aware filtering.

Attorney-reviewed drafts

Draft memo scaffolds and compare ordinance drafts.

Hold candidates

Flag litigation-hold candidates and keep optional memo and hold workpapers.

Current status

Parked, and outside the released suite.

Townlight Legal is a parked foundation. It is not a completed Townlight product, and the Townlight Windows release does not install or support it. Examples and package names in the repository describe the foundation. Do not use them as suite installation instructions.

  • Released Windows suite, current modules and limits
  • Current Townlight products

What it does not do

City attorneys control the source tiers, review drafts and make every legal judgment. It provides no legal advice, legal research service replacement, autonomous legal conclusions, court filing, live language model calls or legal-system connector.
